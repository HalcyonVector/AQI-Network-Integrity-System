2Delhi PM2.5 and regional fire exposure

Delhi PM2.5, daily mean (left axis, µg/m³)Fire exposure index (right axis)Rain, hanging from the top (mm)
Read with care. Delhi PM2.5 stepped up the same day regional fire plumes started arriving, but the days before were rainy (about 9 mm on 26 Sep, 4 mm on 27 Sep) and 30 Sep was the first fully dry day, so rain stopping explains the step without stubble. On rain-free days the association is moderate and not yet significant (about 10 days). Suggestive, not established, and not a share of Delhi's pollution attributable to stubble burning.
