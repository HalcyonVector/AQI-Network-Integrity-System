Delhi PM2.5, fires and weather

Delhi PM2.5 and regional fire exposure
Delhi PM2.5, daily mean (left axis, µg/m³)Fire exposure index (right axis)Rain, hanging from the top (mm)
Read with care. Delhi PM2.5 stepped up the same day regional fire plumes started arriving, but the days before were rainy (about 9 mm on 26 Sep, 4 mm on 27 Sep) and 30 Sep was the first fully dry day, so rain stopping explains the step without stubble. On rain-free days the association is moderate and not yet significant (about 10 days). Suggestive, not established, and not a share of Delhi's pollution attributable to stubble burning.

A flag means "have someone look", not "faulty". A station beside a road or kiln legitimately differs from its neighbours. Flatlines (a sensor repeating one exact value for hours) are the clearest evidence; drift needs weeks of data to confirm.

How to read this page

Sub-index vs concentration. The CPCB feed reports AQI sub-indices; 100 for PM2.5 equals 60 µg/m³. Charts in µg/m³ come from OpenAQ.

Flags are leads, not verdicts. Every statistical result here is exploratory with a few weeks of data; the README lists each limit and every retraction.

Nothing here is a source attribution. The stubble panels show association with fire exposure, not the share of pollution caused by it.
