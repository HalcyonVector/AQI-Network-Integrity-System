2. Delhi PM2.5 and regional fire exposure

Read with care. Delhi PM2.5 stepped up the same day regional fire plumes started arriving, but that is one regime change in about two weeks, and season-onset weather (cooling, shallower mixing) changes at the same time. The association is suggestive, not established; it is not a share of Delhi's pollution attributable to stubble burning.
